# Accountable Sales CRM

Accountable helps a salesperson track business relationships and potential sales.

## Language

**Contact**:
A person the salesperson deals with. A contact can have an organization or be independent of one.

**Deal**:
A potential sale. An active deal has an active organization, an active main contact, or both.

**Call note**:
A record of a call. A call note logged on a contact belongs to that contact.

**Trash**:
The place for deleted records that the salesperson can recover.
